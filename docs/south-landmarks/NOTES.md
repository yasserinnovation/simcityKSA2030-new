# Southern landmarks and Jeddah additions

66 additional southern neighbourhood plots use earth-coloured stepped houses, pale bands, small teal windows, garden terraces and rounded trees. Three small drifting mist clusters frame the highlands, disappearing at close zoom. Reduced-motion preferences freeze the existing cosmetic animation time.

Locally authored landmark kits: Rijal Almaa's nine stone towers with pale quartz-like bands; Shada Palace in Abha's centre; four stepped mud floors for Al-Aan Palace west of Najran; Dosariyah's open courtyard and four corner towers above Jazan's waterfront. Nearby generated mountain geometry is kept clear of scenic landmarks so their massing remains visible. These replace the corresponding generic cluster/heritage models.

Al-Jawhara at King Abdullah Sports City has an open roof, tiered teal bowl, marked field, faceted pale roof, diamond façade pattern and two illustrative practice pitches. It is placed north of the game's Jeddah airport site and inland, between the airport and the already-separated KAEC/Lucid corridor. The illustrated parcel clears project and rail footprints and road corridors. The existing regional layout compresses and displaces distances: this preserves compass relationships, not literal latitude/longitude or a surveyed campus footprint. Geometry is inspired by the supplied stadium photo and [Arup's design description](https://www.arup.com/en-us/projects/king-abdullah-sports-city/).

Nassif House and Beit Al-Matbouli are placed in the existing Al-Balad heritage area south of central Jeddah. Generic roshan houses and decorative neighbourhoods keep clear of their parcels. Nassif has a four-floor-inspired massing, wooden window screens, rooftop pavilion and a tree; Matbouli uses projecting roshan and plaster massing. Exact alley positions, elevations and interiors are illustrative. Sources: [Nassif House, Ministry of Media / Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/nassif-house), [Matbouli in Al-Yaman, official Historic Jeddah site](https://www.visitalbalad.com/en/explore/1615).

Southern reference sources: [Rijal Almaa](https://www.visitsaudi.com/en/aseer/attractions/rijal-almaa-of-aseer), [Shada Palace, national tourism portal](https://my.gov.sa/en/content/tourism), [Al-Aan Palace west of Najran, Najran Municipality](https://www.najran.gov.sa/en/AboutNajran/TouristAttractions), [Dosariyah Castle, official tourism site](https://www.visitsaudi.com/ar/jazan/attraction/al-dosariyah-castle).
